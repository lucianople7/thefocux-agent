# MONETIZATION — turning attention into revenue honestly

World-class operating knowledge for monetizing content, audiences and
products. The FOCUX constitution overrides everything here: money is never
auto-approved, no spam, no extraction, no fake urgency.

## 1. The monetization ladder (from the offer module)
Free value -> lead magnet (email) -> entry offer ($9-49) -> core offer
($99-499) -> premium ($500+). Move people up by DELIVERING at each rung,
never by pressure. Each rung funds and de-risks the next.

## 2. Revenue streams ranked by ownership
1. **Owned** (email list, community, app, digital products): highest LTV,
   algorithm-proof. Build this first.
2. **Rented** (social profiles, marketplace stores): distribution you do not
   own; always capture the audience to owned channels.
3. **Transactional** (affiliate, ads, one-off services): fast cash, low
   asset value. Use to fund owned assets, not as the business.

## 3. Pricing psychology
- Price to the OUTCOME value, not the effort: a $500 course that saves
  $5000/year is cheap; a $10 course that saves nothing is expensive.
- Anchor: show the value stack first (what they get) before the price.
- Tiers create a comparison anchor: 3 tiers, the middle one is the target.
- Perceived value beats cost: framing, proof, guarantee, bonuses.
- Raise prices when demand exceeds capacity; never discount the core offer
  before the guarantee is proven.

## 4. Cost-per-revenue-dollar (the FOCUX survival metric)
- Every revenue stream has a cost (ads, tools, time, fees). Track
  cost/revenue per stream, monthly.
- A stream below 30% cost/revenue is healthy; above 60% is a treadmill.
- The business survives on NET, not gross. The heartbeat tracks runway.

## 5. Honest monetization (constitution Law II/III)
- No fake scarcity, no fabricated social proof, no dark patterns.
- Claims must trace to evidence; refunds honored; guarantees honored.
- Spam signals (unsolicited bulk outreach, fake urgency, bait) are DENIED
  by the gate. Winning honestly compounds; winning by deception is a loss.

## 6. Winner KPIs
- Cost per revenue dollar per stream, conversion per rung, list growth,
  revenue per subscriber, refund rate (should stay low), LTV per stream.
