# SOCIAL MEDIA — platform operating systems

World-class operating knowledge for growing on each platform. The brain
treats each platform as a different MARKET, not a different posting box.

## 1. Platforms are markets with algorithms
- **Instagram**: interest + engagement graph. Reels get reach, carousels
  get saves, stories build intimacy. Post consistency beats bursts.
- **TikTok**: pure discovery. Hook rate + watch time + completion decide
  everything. Duet/stitch to ride trends; originality bonus.
- **YouTube**: search + suggested. Titles/thumbnails ARE the hook; watch
  time and session time rule; long-form builds the asset library.
- **LinkedIn**: professional network. First 2 lines shown; comments drive
  distribution; long-form posts beat articles; engagement is the currency.
- **X**: speed + discussion. The reply-graph drives reach; threads work for
  depth; posting 2-5x/day in your niche timezone wins.
- **Pinterest**: visual search engine, evergreen months-long shelf life,
  keyword-rich pin descriptions.

## 2. The cadence that wins (realistic)
- Minimum viable: 3-5 posts/week on ONE primary platform, 1-2 on others.
- Batch create (one sitting = one week), schedule consistently.
- Post in the audience's active window; test 2 windows, keep the winner.
- Engagement duty: 15-30 min/day replying + commenting (the algorithm
  amplifies accounts that start conversations).

## 3. The growth loop
Create -> post -> measure (hook rate, saves, shares) -> double down ->
repurpose the winners into other formats/platforms -> repeat.
Growth is a LOOP, not a campaign. Momentum compounds.

## 4. Community > audience
- Reply within the first hour (comment velocity feeds the algorithm).
- Ask questions that start threads, not yes/no.
- Save the top 5% of commenters: they are your future buyers.
- DM follow-ups on warm signals (saves + asks) -> lead magnet -> offer.

## 5. Monetization path on social
Attention (followers are a byproduct, not the goal) -> engagement ->
lead magnet (free value) -> email list (OWNED audience, algorithm-proof) ->
offer ladder (free -> lead -> entry -> core -> premium).
Never rent your business to an algorithm: own the list.

## 6. Winner KPIs
- Hook rate and completion (short video), CTR (long), saves/shares (all).
- Cost per engaged view on paid.
- Leads captured per 1000 views (the conversion health number).
- Owned-audience growth (email/subscribers) — the only durable asset.
