# DIGITAL OPPORTUNITIES — the trend -> validation -> launch pipeline

World-class operating knowledge for finding and executing digital
opportunities with evidence, not vibes. THE FOCUX absorbs real signals
(GitHub/HF/X) precisely to feed this pipeline.

## 1. Where opportunities come from (signal sources)
- **Demand spikes**: trending repos/models (absorb), search interest,
  community complaints, "nobody does X" threads, support inbox patterns.
- **Gap analysis**: high demand + low quality supply = opportunity.
- **Migration**: a proven playbook in one niche applied to a hungry niche.
- **Ownership**: your unique skills/data/audience no competitor has.
Score every candidate on: demand, urgency, ability to execute, margin,
competition, durability.

## 2. The validation gate (BEFORE building)
- Talk to 10 real potential buyers; listen for the PROBLEM, not the idea.
- Pre-sell: waitlist, deposit, pre-order page. Paid pre-orders are the only
  proof that matters (intent without payment is a compliment).
- Build the smallest sellable version (landing + offer) and drive 100-200
  clicks of real traffic. Measure the click->buy rate.
- Kill fast: a candidate that fails validation in 2 weeks is dead. Winners
  have no sunk-cost loyalty to losing ideas.

## 3. The launch playbook
- Warm the audience first (3+ content pieces on the problem), then launch
  to the LIST you built while validating.
- Launch stack: announcement + proof + offer + deadline + guarantee.
- Measure: open rate, click rate, conversion, cost per acquisition.
- Post-launch: keep the offer open quietly, iterate on the funnel, capture
  testimonials for the next launch.

## 4. Opportunity velocity (the winner's cadence)
- Run the pipeline continuously: absorb -> score -> validate -> launch ->
  measure -> kill or scale. One validation per week beats one "big idea"
  per quarter.
- Portfolio thinking: 5 candidates -> 2 validate -> 1 launch -> 0.5 become
  real streams. The numbers work if you run the volume.
- Double down on the winner; the market tells you with money, not opinion.

## 5. Winner KPIs
- Candidates scored per week, validation rate, pre-sale conversion,
  days idea->launch (velocity), cost per validated opportunity, revenue per
  launched opportunity, kill rate (honesty: most ideas die — that is the
  system working).
