# CONTENT — the conversion content system

World-class operating knowledge for content that creates attention AND
converts it into business results. This is the WHY behind every content
skill; the skills are the templates, this is the system.

## 1. The job of content
Every piece must do ONE of three jobs, and you must know which one before
writing:
1. **Attract** (cold traffic) — hook + curiosity, zero pitch.
2. **Nurture** (warm) — teach + proof, soft CTA.
3. **Convert** (hot) — offer + urgency + frictionless path.
A piece that tries to do all three does none.

## 2. The hook is 80% of the result
The first 1-3 seconds decide everything (platforms measure hook rate).
- **Patterns that win**: contrarian claim, specific number, open loop,
  "nobody talks about X", before/after, warning, list of mistakes.
- **Rules**: specific beats vague ("5 errores" > "errores"), negative beats
  positive for cold traffic, never bury the hook under a preamble.
- A hook that does not promise a specific payoff is decoration.

## 3. Story > information
Facts are forgotten, stories convert. Structure: tension -> turn -> takeaway.
- Tension: what was at stake.
- Turn: what you learned the hard way.
- Takeaway: one actionable idea the reader can use TODAY.
One story per piece, one takeaway per story.

## 4. Format discipline (per platform native format)
- Short video (TikTok/Reels/Shorts): 15-45s, hook in frame 1, captions on,
  one idea, pattern interrupt.
- Long video (YouTube): hook in 30s, value density, chapters, CTA at end.
- LinkedIn: 100-250 words, first line is the hook, whitespace, one point,
  a question or CTA to drive comments.
- X: 1-2 posts max per idea, thread only when the idea genuinely needs it.
- Newsletter/email: subject line is the hook, 200-400 words, one CTA.

## 5. CTA design
- One CTA per piece, placed AFTER the value is delivered.
- CTA types: follow (cold), comment (engagement), DM/lead magnet (warm),
  buy (hot). Escalate progressively across the content ladder.
- Never CTA before value: it burns trust and the algorithm.

## 6. The system beats the hit
- Volume + iteration beats one viral attempt: 3-5 pieces/week minimum.
- Repurpose EVERY core insight into every format (the FOCUX multiplier).
- Double down on what measures better (hook rate, saves, shares, clicks);
  kill what measures worse. Never defend a piece.

## 7. Winner KPIs
- Hook rate (viewers who stay past 3s) — the #1 leading indicator.
- Saves/shares (value) vs likes (ego). Shares are the growth engine.
- Click-through to the offer — the only metric that pays.
- Cost per lead / cost per revenue dollar on paid amplification.
