# ECOMMERCE — the numbers that decide winners

World-class operating knowledge for ecommerce businesses. Ecommerce is a
MATH game played with products and offers; creativity is in the offer, not
in the spreadsheet.

## 1. The unit economics that must work BEFORE you scale
- **Contribution margin** = price - (COGS + shipping + payment fees +
  ad cost per unit at target CAC). If margin <= 0 at a realistic CAC, no
  amount of traffic fixes the business.
- **Target**: >= 30% contribution margin after ad cost, or a proven LTV
  path that justifies a lower first-purchase margin.
- **Break-even CAC** = contribution margin per sale. Never spend above it
  unless LTV/CAC >= 3 is proven.

## 2. Offer design (the highest-leverage skill in ecommerce)
- Sell the OUTCOME, not the product: "kitchen in 10 minutes" not "knife set".
- Bundle to raise AOV: core product + complementary + free shipping
  threshold (classic: threshold AOV +25%).
- The offer stack: product + bonus + guarantee + urgency + risk reversal.
  A guarantee (refund, "it works or money back") is a conversion machine.
- Price anchoring: show the value stack BEFORE the price.

## 3. Funnel math (AOV / LTV / conversion)
- **AOV** = total revenue / orders. Raise it with bundles, tiers, upsells.
- **Conversion rate** benchmarks: cold paid traffic 1-3%, warm/retargeting
  3-10%, email/owned 5-15%. Compare against YOUR baseline, not the industry.
- **LTV** = AOV x purchase frequency x gross margin x retention period.
- One-time buyer -> repeat buyer is THE profit lever: email flows, loyalty,
  subscription or refill offers.

## 4. Product-market fit first
- Niche + problem + willingness to pay: validate with pre-orders, waitlist,
  or a minimum viable listing BEFORE inventory.
- Score the niche: pain intensity, frequency, audience size, competition
  gap, willingness to pay, margin room. 4+ out of 6 = go.
- Positioning: one clear enemy (the old way, the status quo, a rival).

## 5. The winning stack
- Offer (outcome + bundle + guarantee) -> landing page (one message, one
  CTA, proof, no nav) -> payment flow (minimize steps, trust badges) ->
  post-purchase (upsell + thank-you + email flows) -> retention (win-back,
  subscription, referrals).
- Test ONE variable at a time: headline, price point, bundle, guarantee.
  Kill what measures worse within 2 weeks; never defend a losing variant.

## 6. Winner KPIs
- Contribution margin %, break-even CAC, AOV, LTV/CAC, repeat purchase rate,
  conversion rate per traffic source, cost per revenue dollar.
- The scoreboard that matters: **profit per order**, not revenue.
